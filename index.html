📖 How to Use

👁️ Vision Mode Extraction

Adopt(k) v2.1 uses AI vision to actually SEE your PDF, just like ChatGPT does. The AI visually examines checkboxes instead of relying on text extraction.

Why Vision? Text extraction often loses checkbox marks. Vision mode sees [✓] and [X] directly.

Steps

1. Select Claude Sonnet (best) or Gemini for vision mode

2. Upload your adoption agreement PDF

3. Click Process - the AI will visually analyze each page

4. Review results - Form of Business, Age Requirements, etc. now detected correctly

✨ What's New in v2.1

👁️ Vision Mode

The AI now SEES your PDF visually, examining checkboxes just like a human would. No more lost checkbox marks!

Fixed: Form of Business, Entity Type, Minimum Age, Service Requirement - all the checkbox fields now work correctly.

How It Works

Instead of extracting text (which loses checkboxes), we send the actual PDF to Claude/Gemini which can see the document visually.

Best Model: Claude Sonnet 4.5 has the best vision accuracy for adoption agreements.
